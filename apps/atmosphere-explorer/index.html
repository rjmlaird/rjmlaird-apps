Earth’s atmosphere: surface to the edge of space

Explore the troposphere, stratosphere and mesosphere on a true 0–100 km scale, then click to zoom into the thermosphere and low-Earth-orbit region. Select any layer for its radiation, temperature, pressure and spaceflight context.

Click the diagram to zoom. The first view uses an undistorted 0–100 km altitude scale; clicking switches to a compressed 0–1,000 km view that includes the ISS orbit.

Reading the diagram: the 0–100 km view is drawn with a consistent kilometres-per-pixel scale. The zoomed view compresses altitudes above 100 km so the thermosphere, Kármán line and ISS orbit can be shown together. Values are illustrative standard-atmosphere figures and vary with latitude, season and weather.
