Earth Interior Explorer

Inside Earth: crust, mantle and core

Explore Earth’s major internal layers, their composition, temperature, pressure and state. Switch views to see seismic waves, plate tectonics, heat flow and the geodynamo that generates Earth’s magnetic field.

Reading the diagrams: the cutaway is not to a single linear depth scale—the crust is vastly thinner than the mantle, so it is exaggerated to remain visible. Depths, temperatures and pressures are approximate global averages; they vary substantially by location.
